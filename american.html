St. Elmo Steak House
127 South Illinois Street
Indianapolis, IN 46225
Phone - (317) 635-0636

St. Elmo Steak House started in 1902 when Joe Stahr opened a saloon downtown called Joe Stahr's Tavern. The name was shortened to St. Elmo later on. It is best known for its shrimp cocktail with the strong horseradish sauce, and the James Beard Foundation named it an America's Classic. The original brick building and tiger oak bar are still there.
