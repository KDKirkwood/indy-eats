Imperial Palace was named the best Chinese restaurant in Indiana by Love Food. People like the garlic chicken, beef with broccoli and orange shrimp, and the crab rangoon and egg rolls are popular starters. It also has weekday lunch sets that come with rice, a spring roll and an entree.
