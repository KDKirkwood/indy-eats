Mama Carolla's
1031 East 54th Street
Indianapolis, IN 46220
Phone - (317) 259-9412

Mama Carolla's opened in 1997 in an old stucco house near the Monon Trail. Each room in the house is a small dining room, which gives it a cozy feel. Popular dishes include the steamed mussels and Holly's artichoke fritters. It takes reservations on a limited basis and seats the rest of the tables first come, first served.
